GEMINI RECOMMENDED CONCEPT

- Outgoing text moves away/depth-fades; incoming text rotates to dead-center/front and returns to crisp readable state.

MOTION DIRECTION PROPOSED BY GEMINI

- Scene perspective with phrases fixed around an implied radius/rotor.

- Autoplay with pause on hover/focus/touch; manual advancement supported.
