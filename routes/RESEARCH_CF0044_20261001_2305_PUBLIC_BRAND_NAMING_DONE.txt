For each participant:
1. Show the written name for 3–5 seconds; ask them to pronounce it.
2. Say the name aloud without showing it; ask them to spell it.
3. Put it into both exact contexts:
   - “Yo, I built _____. I’ll send you the link.”
   - “Humber is piloting _____ with students.”
4. Ask: “What do you think this product does?” before giving a description.
5. Ask for immediate unwanted associations: childish, medical, finance, logistics, K–12-only, corporate-software, AI-startup, religious/spiritual, or other.
6. Record exact mispronunciations/misspellings and first associations; do not collapse them into a single preference score.

A candidate should advance only if people can say it, spell it, remember it, and place it naturally in both peer and institutional speech without explanation.
