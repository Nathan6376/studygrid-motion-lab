======================================================================
PATTERNS EXPLICITLY REJECTED
======================================================================

1. Product noun + synthetic direction/home suffix:
   Study/Class/Course/Lesson/Learn/Sense + -forth/-ward/-stead/-path/-line/etc.
   This is the exact failure mode that produced Senseward/Classforth/Plainforth/Coursestead/Lessonforth.

2. Semantic rewrites of StudyGrid:
   anything whose value proposition is effectively “study direction,” “class path,” “learning signal,” “course map,” or “what next” compressed into a name.

3. Generated EdTech cadence without literal education words:
   smooth vowel-heavy pseudo-Latin names, obvious AI-startup morphology, or repeated -ora/-ira/-iva/-ely/-ly families that look machine-generated even when category-neutral.

4. Fake ownability through obscure spelling:
   names that become unique only by violating normal English spelling or requiring a pronunciation explanation.

5. Names carrying the wrong category signal:
   pseudo-medical, fintech, crypto, logistics, enterprise-infrastructure, or children’s-app sound.
