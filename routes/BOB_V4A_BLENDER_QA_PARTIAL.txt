EXACT DEFECTS FOUND / DISPOSITION
1. STUDY 2A — WRONG PARENT-INVERSE / LARGE ORBIT — FIXED IN BRANCH.
   Original child setup applied hinge parent + matrix_parent_inverse, then assigned child local location (0.55,0,0.55). The parent inverse cancelled the intended hinge-frame relationship and produced a large orbit instead of a local face-edge fold. Analytically, the child centre travels approximately (-2.30,0,3.30) -> (-0.305,0,2.515) -> (0.55,0,0.55) while the parent is at (-2.8,0,0).
   Fix: remove the parent inverse; define child centre directly in hinge-local space as ((L+GAP)/2,0,-L/2). Final centre becomes (-1.7,0,0), preserving the 0.10 resting gap. Sampled oriented-box checks over the full -90° -> 0° sweep found no parent/child intersection; minimum broad-face separation is about 0.05 during the arc.
