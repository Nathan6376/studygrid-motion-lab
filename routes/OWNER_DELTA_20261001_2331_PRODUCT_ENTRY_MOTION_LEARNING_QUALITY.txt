OWNER INTENT
Nathan is not asking for another cosmetic patch. He wants the next frontend pass redesigned as a coherent premium product experience before Bob mutates code.

1. TWO FIRST-CLASS LEARNING CONTEXTS
- StudyGrid must support both:
  A. class/course learning; and
  B. general/self-directed learning outside a class, e.g. learning something for work or personal development.
- These are distinct contexts sharing the same underlying StudyGrid learning engine.
- Public/onboarding copy cannot imply the entire product is only for classes.
- “Your class, easier to learn” may remain useful inside the class-specific branch, but it should not be the universal product promise on the top-level public entry.
- Entry/onboarding should make the choice understandable without making the product feel split into two unrelated apps.
- Class flow still needs strong class-code / classes / course context and should feel intentionally designed, not like a form page.

2. MOTION / VISUAL QUALITY BAR
- Preserve the current warm StudyGrid design language; do not replace it with generic purple/blue AI gradients.
- Raise the visual system from “nicely designed modern website” to premium, futuristic, motion-rich product experience.
- Current owner perception: present site feels closer to 2016-modern / pencil-and-paper UI than a 2026 premium AI-assisted learning product.
- Motion must be meaningful, responsive and integrated into state changes, not decorative gimmicks.
- Reuse already supplied design inputs: Apple/Emil principles, Thinking Orbs state-motion lesson, Claude/Opus source-first motion workflow, calm/premium/intentional motion, reduced-motion support, interruptible/reversible interaction where relevant, no target/focus loss.
- Hover/press reactions Nathan already likes should be retained and expanded into a coherent motion system.
- Landing 1→2→3 StudyGrid loop still needs continuous cycling as previously directed, but exact choreography is a design decision first.

3. LEARNING COPY — SIMPLE ENGLISH
- Remove internal learning-science jargon from learner-facing copy.
- Nathan explicitly rejects phrases like “Start retrieval” and “before retrieving it.”
- Use natural language such as “Try it from memory”, “Test yourself”, “Try it on your own”, “See what you remember”, etc., based on context.
- “Retrieval” may remain an internal engine/analytics term, not normal learner UI copy.
- Buttons must say plainly what will actually happen.

4. LEARN FLOW MUST FEEL ALIVE AND ACTUALLY WORK
- “Start learning” cannot appear to do nothing or merely light/highlight a card with no obvious content transition.
- Starting learning should create a clear, meaningful transition into the actual explanation/lesson experience.
- Explanation, example, guided practice and independent practice must be genuinely usable and visibly open/advance, not static card labels.
- General Study must also be a real first-class flow, not a fixture/toast placeholder.

5. QUESTION QUALITY / DIFFICULTY
- StudyGrid questions must test understanding, not the learner’s ability to spot one serious answer among obviously absurd distractors.
- Example owner complaint: hot-pursuit/residence multiple choice is too obvious when one answer is plainly correct and others are obviously unrelated/wrong.
- Guided-practice example with “What grade will I receive?” as the wrong option is especially unacceptable because it gives away the answer rather than testing the concept.
- Distractors should be plausible confusions a learner could realistically make, source-grounded when possible, and close enough in form/semantic category that the learner must actually understand the concept.
- Avoid giveaway wording, joke/irrelevant distractors, wildly different answer length/structure, or choices that can be eliminated without subject knowledge.
- Difficulty should come from discriminating between nearby concepts, applying rules to facts, sequencing/conditions/exceptions, and explaining why — not from trick wording.
- The engine should own/question-generation quality rules rather than relying on generic “AI makes a quiz” behaviour.

6. CURRENT v7.22 SOURCE OBSERVATIONS CONFIRMING THE DELTA
- Public welcome copy is still class-only: “Your class, easier to learn” / “Learn the class. Be ready for the test.”
- General Study exists after sign-in but its Continue button only displays a fixture toast saying a future adapter would provide real material/action.
- Current Learn copy includes “Understand home-entry authority before retrieving it” and “Start retrieval”.
- “Start learning” is wired only to open the explanation panel in-place; this is technically functional but visually weak enough to read as no-op to the owner.
- Guided practice currently uses “What grade will I receive?” as a distractor.
- Current hot-pursuit multiple choice includes distractors that are obviously non-hot-pursuit scenarios, so the item can be solved by elimination without strong domain understanding.
