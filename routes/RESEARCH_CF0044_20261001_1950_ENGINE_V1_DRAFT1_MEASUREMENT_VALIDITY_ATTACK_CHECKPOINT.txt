Material findings established:
1. Draft 1 materially fixes Draft 0's category-collapse problem, but the five-layer chain is still incomplete because observations are policy/task-generated rather than neutral. Draft 1 needs an explicit MEASUREMENT DESIGN / WARRANT layer before interpretation and an ACTION-OUTCOME / SELECTION-MECHANISM record around policy execution.
2. Learning Claim needs explicit generalization boundary/conditions; task→claim mapping plus rationale is not itself a validity argument.
3. Raw response, score/rubric output, and evidence interpretation must remain distinct. Constructed-response scores—human or AI—are scored interpretations with scorer uncertainty/provenance, not observation facts.
4. Transfer cannot be inferred from task-family diversity; it requires novel/generalization evidence. Repeated identical/near-identical items are dependent observations and must not be counted as independent confirmation.
5. Coached/scaffolded success is a different evidence channel from unaided performance. Accommodation and language conditions must be represented as validity context without becoming competence penalties or trait inferences.
6. Retention should remain construct-specific and time-indexed; Engine v1 cannot scientifically fit learner-specific memory parameters from a ~20-student beta.
7. Rule-based next-action policy can be beta-tested as transparent decision logic, but several priorities are only plausible hypotheses. `introduced` cannot be a universal gate because bounded pretesting/diagnostic use can be pedagogically valid; `diversity` is not universally beneficial; `urgency` is a curriculum priority signal, not learning-effect evidence.
8. Adaptive selection makes later training data policy-dependent. Candidate set, selected action, behavior-policy version, support/feedback exposure, task-family exposure, source/mapping/scorer versions and—where randomized—selection propensity must be logged or later causal/off-policy claims become unsafe.
9. A ~20-student + one-professor beta can test data/ontology coherence, mapping disagreements, scorer agreement, explanation comprehension, usability/trust, override behavior, item/accessibility failures and descriptive within-beta patterns. It cannot establish efficacy, calibrated mastery thresholds, fairness, stable psychometrics, causal policy superiority or institutional generalizability.
10. Scientifically unsafe areas are being isolated for terminal return: universal mastery/sufficiency thresholds, inferred hard prerequisites, universal retention models, AI-scored learner updates without validated scoring, causal instructor labels, timing/accommodation/language penalties, learned policy/RL, naive model training from adaptive logs, and fairness/efficacy claims from the beta.
