STUDYGRID V4A — DOORWAY CUBE-SELECTION POLICY FIXTURE

Scope
- Isolated policy/test fixture only. It does not integrate with StudyGrid, Blender camera motion, hinge mechanics, tutorial handoff, backend, R5/R6, Supabase, deployment, canonical G, or final choreography.

Locked behaviour
1. Doorway target selection is logically separate from camera movement and object scale.
2. Runtime may select among the currently eligible cube IDs.
3. When more than one cube is eligible and the immediately previous selection is still eligible, that cube is excluded for the next run.
4. With one eligible cube, repeat is unavoidable and therefore allowed.
5. A stale/invalid previous cube does not constrain the current run.
6. Tests may pass an integer seed for deterministic replay. Future runtime integration may pass a long-lived RNG instead. Passing both is an error.
7. Eligible IDs must be non-empty and unique.

Assumptions deferred to integration
- The product layer owns which cubes are eligible for a run and how selection history is persisted.
- This fixture does not choose a visual face, camera path, hinge variant, reveal order, or tutorial timing.
- Statistical distribution beyond uniform choice within the permitted pool is not specified here.
