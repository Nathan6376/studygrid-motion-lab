STUDYGRID BOB Q-B8 — GLOBAL MOTION-INVARIANT SCANNER

PURPOSE
Reusable candidate-neutral/choreography-neutral QA scanner for the final selected-candidate integration before render. It reports prohibited motion cheats and camera drift without choosing A/C or deciding reveal order.

CHECKS
1. Governed object scale
- rejects keyframed/driven scale on configured cube/G-seed object variables;
- optional strict mode can also reject direct scale assignment after object construction.

2. Fixed-lens camera
- rejects keyframed/driven lens, angle, angle_x or angle_y on configured fixed-lens camera variables;
- rejects multiple/nonconstant direct focal assignments that imply focal drift.

3. Hidden-mechanics visibility/opacity
- recognizes set_linear_visibility(...) windows for configured hidden-path objects;
- rejects undeclared visibility windows and undeclared hide/opacity keyframes;
- includes a conservative lexical alpha-keyframe fallback.

4. Final cube structure
- optional final-state manifest validates expected cube count, equal dimensions and declared adjacency air gaps;
- rejects dimension drift, invalid adjacency references, overlap and configured minimum-gap collapse.

REPORT
Schema: studygrid.motion_invariant_scan.v1
Each finding includes code, severity, subject, source line where available, frame/time where recoverable, and detail. Overall status is FAIL on errors, HOLD when required final-state evidence is absent, otherwise PASS.

SYNTHETIC VERIFICATION
The companion verifier covers:
- clean positive case;
- animated-scale negative;
- animated-lens/FOV negative;
- hidden-visibility-window negative;
- dimension-drift negative;
- gap-collapse negative;
- missing-state HOLD case.
All seven cases passed in Bob's Q-B8 execution environment.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- Static AST analysis cannot prove rendered appearance, collision-free motion, occlusion quality, or browser/WebGL parity.
- Final cube dimension/gap validation requires a caller-supplied final-state manifest; absence produces HOLD rather than fabricated evidence.
- Dynamic exec/eval, generated code, custom handlers or drivers hidden behind unrecognised helper layers can require Blender-runtime instrumentation.
- Visibility exceptions must be explicitly declared in config; otherwise hidden-path visibility animation is rejected.
- Axis-gap validation assumes axis-aligned final-state cube dimensions/centres supplied in the manifest. Rotated intermediate OBB collision remains a separate mechanics/render QA concern.

BOUNDARIES
Isolated tool only. No main/product/workflow/R5/R6/Supabase/backend/auth/deploy/canonical-G/final-choreography mutation. No candidate winner or preference is encoded.
