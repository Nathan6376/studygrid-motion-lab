Future QA disposition:

- BLOCKED: exact candidate/repaired baseline cannot be identified; functional repair freeze is unresolved; candidate cannot be meaningfully exercised; test evidence is unavailable; or a structural failure prevents the required QA paths.

Severity:
- P0: security/privacy/authority failure or real-system mutation outside scope. Immediate stop/escalation.
- P1: hard product/IA/truth/accessibility/function acceptance failure. Candidate cannot pass the visual/IA gate.
- P2: material quality/clarity/motion/focus defect requiring amendment before owner acceptance, but does not by itself prove corrupt learning truth.
- P3: polish/consistency issue; record, but it does not block a bounded local acceptance unless it accumulates into a material usability failure.
- HOLD: evidence cannot be cleared in the current environment (physical iPad/Safari/Pencil, VoiceOver/AT, hosted-origin or Nathan visual judgement).

Evidence forms:
- SRC = source/diff inspection.
- AUTO = unit/component/provider automated test.
- BROW = browser test with route/action/assertion + console/network capture.
- REC = screenshot/video/trace showing motion/visual sequence.
- HOST = exact Vercel/hosted-preview evidence tied to commit/build identity.
- PHYS = physical iPad/Safari/touch/Pencil evidence.
- AT = physical VoiceOver/assistive-technology evidence.
